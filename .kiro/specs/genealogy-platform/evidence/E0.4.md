## Decisions

- The deliverable is **platform DPIA scope only**. Customer-specific DPIA for on-premise deployments is explicitly out of scope and called out in §2; E0.5 ADR on customer DPA template is in §14 open question #7.
- Every numeric / policy value is flagged `DRAFT` and routed to an ADR; the document records **no ADR-class decision** of its own. This honours `agent-execution.md` §2 "không tự chọn công nghệ … cần ADR".
- Default of `legal.dna.enabled = false` and `legal.public_sharing.enabled = false` in §12 was chosen because the DPIAs in §7 and §9 explicitly conclude "DNA MUST NOT be enabled … until ADR E0.5-04 closes and ADR E10.1 signs off". This is a privacy posture, not a technology decision, so it does not require its own ADR.
- The cross-region rule (§3) is taken verbatim from `scale-and-slo.md` §7 to keep residency consistent across deliverables; the legal-mechanism behind it (SCC / IDTA / adequacy) is left to ADR E0.5-07.
- The deletion-evidence design (§13) deliberately retains **only a SHA-256 of ciphertext and a pointer to the audit entry** — never the deleted payload. This satisfies `requirements.md` §6.5 ("tạo deletion evidence không chứa nội dung đã xóa") and GDPR Art. 17 (right to erasure) without creating a parallel copy of the data.
- The DNA-specific deletion-evidence assertions (§13.3) mirror the E10.5 workflow obligations so that E10.5 can take this section as the binding spec.
- No assumption was made about jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction specifics beyond what's already in the design (`scale-and-slo.md` §7 + `design.md` §1 platform stack). Anything more specific is parked in §14 for ADR closure.

## Security and privacy

- Tenant isolation: PASS — DPIA §6 enumerates 8 risks; the highest residual (`On-premise misconfig drops RLS` T-06) is bounded by E2.1 preflight + E1.2 RLS test in CI; ADR E0.5-02 captures the topology choice.
- Authorization/ABAC: PASS — DPIA §7 + §9 require ABAC obligations (`redact`, `watermark`, `audit`) before projection; the policy-model hooks from `design.md` §6.2 are referenced via §15 cross-reference map.
- Secret/PII/DNA leakage: PASS — §11 declares the forbidden payload classes and ties them to CI regex gates; §13 deletion evidence never carries content; SHA-256 of ciphertext is the only artefact retained.
- Dependency/config scan: N/A — pure Markdown at this stage; the security CI gates referenced in §11 will be set up by E1.6 (Semgrep, Trivy, Gitleaks, Grype, Checkov).
- Resilience / chaos: PASS — §13 includes a nightly reconciliation Temporal workflow that asserts no legal hold remains active for a record already deleted; this protects against hold-bypass races.
- Residency safety: PASS — §3 binds each tenant to one residency cluster at onboarding, immutable; cross-region replication is allowed only within the same jurisdiction class and gated by `legal.data_residency.allowlist` + `legal.cross_region_transfer.enabled`.
