## Decisions

- **ADR-E0.5-06 (OpenFGA + ABAC): ACCEPTED** — OpenFGA decides the
  relationship verdict, ABAC overlay (living / minor / DNA / consent)
  decides the contextual deny. The orchestrator chains them with a
  stale-permission-version short-circuit.
- **ADR-E0.5-09 (Permission-version binding): ACCEPTED** —
  `permissionVersionBindingEnforced=true` + `permissionCacheMaxVersionWindow=5` +
  `tombstoneSeconds=90 ≥ 3 × maxAgeSeconds=30` enforce the
  freshness window per R11.2 + NFR1.
- **ADR-E0.5-15 (DNA bucket shield): ACCEPTED** —
  `dnaBucketAccess=FORBIDDEN` + DNA bucket prefixes enforced at
  every query + alert + saved-search path.
- **Cross-service references are opaque (design.md §5.1):
  ACCEPTED** — `crossServiceReferencesAreOpaque=true` +
  `crossServiceReferencesRequirePublisherResolution=true`; the
  orchestrator never resolves opaque ids (that lives in the
  album / reconciliation worker).
